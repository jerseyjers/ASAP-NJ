Straight talk: This is for legal hunters recovering legally harvested game, with permission on the land we fly. We will not help with poaching, trespass, or anything off the books. You stay responsible for seasons, tags, and NJ hunting rules.

Why thermal in the woods

A downed deer still holds heat for a while. In thick cover, that signature can show up on infrared long after RGB cameras and naked eyes fail. We grid or track the search area you give us— last blood, shot direction, bedding thickets—and mark candidates so you can walk in with a GPS pin, not a guess.

Best fit

  • Legal shot, animal left the open
  • Blood trail lost in brush or swamp edge
  • Thick pine / oak / mixed cover
  • Low light when thermal contrast is better
  • You have landowner permission (or own the land)

What we need from you

  • Approx. shot location & time
  • Direction of travel / last blood
  • Property access & who owns it
  • Phone-free is fine—use the form; we’ll email back
  • Honest status: tag, season, legal harvest

What this is not

Not guaranteed

Canopy density, hours since shot, rain, and distance all cut odds. We maximize the search—we don’t sell miracles.

Not a wildlife survey product

This page is about ethical recovery after a legal harvest, not spotting live game for hunting advantage in illegal ways.

Not trespass aviation

We need clear permission for the property and a plan that respects neighbors, roads, and airspace.

Not a substitute for good tracking

Bring your story from the ground—last blood, direction, time. Thermal works best when we start smart, not random.

Also flying near the woods

Same thermal toolkit powers solar, HVAC, roofs, and claim sites. If you’re a hunter who also owns property or manages land, ask about combining recovery readiness with inspection work later.

FAQ

How does thermal drone deer recovery work?

We fly a thermal search over the area you describe after a legal harvest, looking for heat signatures that match a downed deer under cover, then pass candidate spots for ground recovery.

Is this for any deer search?

Legal harvests only, with landowner permission and compliance with hunting law. No poaching support. Ever.

Can you guarantee a find?

No. Conditions vary. We fly to improve your odds when tracking from the ground has stalled.

How fast can you respond?

Depends on weather, airspace, and schedule. Use the form ASAP after the shot—time works against residual heat.

Recovery brief

Location, time of shot, and access—faster form = better odds while residual heat lasts.
