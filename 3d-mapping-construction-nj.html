FAQ

What do engineers and GCs get from a mapping flight?

Scoped deliverables—dense capture for 3D/ortho-style products, progress packages, and meeting-ready stills. We align formats before the flight.

Can you fly the same site as the project develops?

Yes. Field → office → progress is the core loop. Same site, updated reality, consistent handoff.

Do you offer RTK for higher accuracy mapping?

Fixed-base NTRIP on the Toms River (instant free seat or Pro upgrade for full workdays; any NTRIP client). On-site RTK quoted for remote projects. Ask when you request a mapping job—or open the RTK section.

Is this a licensed land survey?

No. ASAP NJ provides aerial capture and documentation products for project use. Boundary, legal surveys, and sealed work stay with licensed surveyors when the law requires them.
